How to Use AABStudio

From first project to exported video — a complete guide.

1

Create a Project

From your Dashboard click + New Project. All your work — script, scenes, recordings, AI videos — saves automatically to the cloud.

2

Write Your Script

Go to Script, write your content, then click Split into Scenes. Each scene becomes a separate recording clip.

3

Record with Teleprompter

Click Studio. Allow camera/mic. Your script appears as a teleprompter. Use ⏺ REC to record, ← → to navigate scenes.

  • 💡 Word mode — Karaoke word-by-word highlight
  • ⏱ Sync — Start recording + TP together
  • QR Remote — Control from your phone
4

Generate AI Presenter Videos

From Scenes, click 🤖 AI Video. Upload a reference photo (optional), choose your AI voice and background, then click ⚡ Generate All. Videos auto-load into Media Bin and Timeline.

5

Edit in the Edit Suite

All clips load into the Media Bin and Timeline automatically. Professional tools:

  • Right-click clip — Split, Duplicate, Trim, Delete
  • Right-click track — Add Video/Audio/Image/Text
  • C key — Razor cut at playhead
  • Space — Play/Pause
6

Export

Click ↓ Export. Choose resolution (720p/1080p/4K) and format. Your video downloads automatically.
