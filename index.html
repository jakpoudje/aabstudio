Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect: (a) Account information — your email address and password hash; (b) Project data — titles, scene scripts, and settings; (c) Payment information — processed securely by Stripe, we never store card details; (d) Anonymous usage analytics to improve the service.

2. Video Recordings

Videos recorded in the Recording Studio are processed entirely in your browser and stored locally on your device. We do not upload or store your webcam recordings. AI-generated videos are processed by third-party services (HeyGen, D-ID) under their own privacy policies.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to: provide and maintain the Platform; process payments; improve features; send service communications. We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

4. Data Storage and Security

Account data is stored securely via Supabase (AWS EU). We use HTTPS encryption in transit and encryption at rest. Access to user data is restricted to authorised personnel only.

5. Third-Party Services

AABStudio integrates with: Supabase (auth/database), Stripe (payments), Anthropic Claude (AI assistance), ElevenLabs (voice), HeyGen and D-ID (AI video generation), Imgur (image hosting). Each operates under its own privacy policy.

6. Camera and Microphone

Camera and microphone access is used solely for local recording in the Recording Studio. No camera or microphone data is transmitted to our servers.

7. Your Rights (GDPR)

You have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below. You may delete your account at any time from Account Settings.

8. Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication sessions only. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

9. Data Retention

Your account data is retained while your account is active. Payment records are retained as required by law. You may request full deletion at any time.

10. Contact

For privacy questions: privacy@aabstudio.ai
